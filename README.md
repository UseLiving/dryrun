# DRYRUN

Real coins. Paper hands.

**Live site:** https://useliving.github.io/dryrun/  
**X:** https://x.com/UseDryrun

## What it is
Paper trading for Solana memecoins. Start with 100 pretend SOL, catch coins the second they launch, trade them at the live price with a 1.25% fee each side, and see what every round made. No wallet, no deposit, nothing real moves.

## What's on the site
- A working demo desk: Pulse (New pairs, Final stretch, Migrated), quick buy and filters
- Trade view with 1-second candles and market, limit, stop and trailing orders
- Portfolio with equity curve, trade history and a shareable round card
- Wallet tracker with live notes, and a daily leaderboard with a UTC reset countdown
- Pixel risograph look with paper planes that react to your cursor

Single self-contained `index.html`.

> Preview build: coins, prices, wallets and the leaderboard on the site are simulated. Memecoins are extremely risky. Not financial advice.
